## Open

- **A mined block can exceed the block buffer.** `OnTipChanged` fills the block with `TXPool.GetTXs(block.Buffer.Length)`, so the TXs alone may use all of `SizeBlockMax`, and the header, TX count and coinbase come on top. With 1 MB or more in the pool, `Block.Serialize` throws on `CopyTo`, so mining fails on every tip change, after the anchor TX for the lost block has already been stored, and the exception disconnects the peer whose block changed the tip.
